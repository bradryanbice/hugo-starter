An ordinary page. A few things worth knowing:

- Images go in a page bundle next to the page, or in `assets/`, never in
  `static/`. They need alt text, and the build fails if it is missing.
- The `callout`, `figure` and `table` shortcodes are available.
- Everything here is checked: accessibility, colour contrast in both schemes,
  the document head, and page weight.
